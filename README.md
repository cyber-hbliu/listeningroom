# stay tuned
I've been digging out albums and tracks for years, and I'm obsessed with all genres. Then it turns out that my playlist of liked music is overloaded. I barely share my good finds or listenings on social media, but one day I came up with this idea in an online listening room. It's clean and easy to feature my weekly music discovery, particularly my fav tracks. I consider this a cyberspace euphony journaling - well, it might be fuzzy and glitchy sometimes - at least good things hype me up.
